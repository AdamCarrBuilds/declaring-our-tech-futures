Declaring Our Tech Futures

Who gets to decide what comes next?

AI is opening a new chapter in our technological lives. The decisions being made now will shape how we work, learn, relate to one another, and participate in public life. We believe people should have a meaningful voice in those decisions.

Declaring Our Tech Futures is an emerging coalition bringing people together to imagine the technological futures we want, and explore how we can shape them together.

Get involved Follow along

The opportunity

There is no shortage of conversation about the technological futures we fear. We want to create space for people to articulate what they hope for, consider difficult choices together, and help guide what comes next.

2027

We are exploring a national citizens’ assembly on AI in 2027: a process in which people from different backgrounds could learn, deliberate, and develop recommendations for our shared technological future. Alongside that exploration, we are building relationships across communities, civic organizations, and the technology world.

This work is at an early stage. We are inviting partners, advisors, and supporters to help shape the questions, the process, and the pathways through which public participation can influence real decisions.

The history

Thirty years after John Perry Barlow’s A Declaration of the Independence of Cyberspace (opens in a new tab), we are examining the lessons of the last three decades we can't afford to lose: what we want to carry forward, what needs rethinking, and how those lessons can help us shape the future in the age of AI. Amelia Rose Barlow and Amanda Arch are co-stewards of this initiative.

Mei Lin Fung

Co-founder, People-Centered Internet.

Mei Lin Fung is a globally recognized technology and policy leader, serving as Vice Chair of the UN AI for Good Impact Steering Committee and Chair of the IEEE SSIT Sustainability Committee. She co-founded the People-Centered Internet with Vint Cerf and co- founded the Nehitek Foundation with Michael MacFarland. Her career spans roles at Shell, Intel, and Oracle, where she was part of the original team that pioneered Customer Relationship Management systems.

She has co-authored numerous G7 Think Tank (Think7) policy briefs (2022–2025) on MSME financing, digital public infrastructure, and AI governance. Mei Lin served as socio-technical lead for the US Department of Defense’s Federal Health Futures project under Lt. Gen. Bruce Green, bringing Douglas Engelbart’s networked improvement communities into federal medicine.

Her current work emphasizes using Digital Public Infrastructure and securitization to finance MSMEs globally, while advancing inclusive science and AI governance. She serves as a bridge between technical communities (IEEE, ACM, ITU) with policy leaders worldwide.. she is coordinating editor of the upcoming Springer Nature book on AI and the Future of Medical Decision Making : A Hippocratic Oath for the AI Age
